Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Boardcut is a free, open source app for designing wardrobes, closets and shelving. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, no server that receives your projects, no advertising and no analytics.

What is stored, and where

Your projects and preferences (units, the last settings you chose) are stored in your browser, on your device. We don't receive them and can't see them. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose Settings → Google Drive → Connect, Boardcut signs you in to Google and keeps your projects as files in a Boardcut folder in your own Google Drive, updating them as you work.

  • It asks only for the drive.file permission: Boardcut can see and change only the files and folder it creates there, or the folder you pick for it. It cannot read the rest of your Drive.
  • Your projects go straight from your browser to Google. They don't pass through any server of ours.
  • The sign-in token is kept in the page's memory only and is gone when you close the tab. What Boardcut remembers in your browser is which folder you chose and which Drive file belongs to which project.
  • Deleting a project in Boardcut moves its file to your Drive trash.
  • Disconnect any time in the same Settings page, or remove Boardcut's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Boardcut's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to sync your projects for you. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Other services

The app loads its own files and, only if you connect Google Drive, scripts from Google. It has no analytics, trackers or third-party cookies. Whoever hosts a copy of the app (for boardcut.app, Cloudflare) may keep ordinary server logs of page requests, such as IP address, as any web host does.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above changes. The source code is public at github.com/dtsakadze/room-designer, where you can ask questions or report a problem through Issues.
